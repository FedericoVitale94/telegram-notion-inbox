# Voice and style rules for drafts

Copy this file to `prompts/voice.md` and rewrite it in your own words. The bot sends it to the model
with every draft, together with your best past scripts (if `[archive]` has a `script_property`).
The more specific the rules, the closer the drafts get to your voice.

## Format
- Spoken script for a short vertical video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts).
- One narrator, speaking directly to the viewer as "you".
- Short sentences, one idea per breath. Written for the ear, not the eye.

## Structure
1. Hook in the first sentence: a surprising fact or question, no greeting.
2. Context: two or three sentences that make the situation feel familiar.
3. Build-up: two or three details in growing order of interest.
4. Turn: the moment that changes how the story reads.
5. Close: an open question to the viewer.

## Rules
- Only facts from the material. If the material is thin, write a shorter draft and say what is missing.
- Rewrite in your own words: never translate or copy the source video.
- Attribute claims you cannot verify ("some say", "according to the video").
- No hype words ("insane", "you won't believe").
